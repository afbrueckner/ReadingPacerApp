Cognitive Reading Pacer — Field Manual

Catholic Middle School Edition • Research-Backed Fluency & Comprehension Protocol

1. Key Metrics & Definitions

WPM (Words Per Minute): Pure physical tracking speed across lines of text (Words / Seconds × 60).

Comprehension %: Accuracy score on 3-question verification check (100%, 67%, 33%, or 0%).

ERR (Effective Reading Rate): Calculated as WPM × Comprehension %.

ΔERR (Growth Delta): True cognitive growth measured between Form A and Form B.
