📖 Educator & Parent Field Guide

Cognitive Reading Pacer — Field Manual

Catholic Middle School Edition • Research-Backed Fluency & Comprehension Protocol

1. Key Metrics & Definitions

WPM (Words Per Minute): Pure physical tracking speed across lines of text ($\text{Words} / \text{Seconds} \times 60$).

Comprehension %: Accuracy score on 3-question verification check ($100\%$, $67\%$, $33\%$, or $0\%$).

ERR (Effective Reading Rate): Calculated as $\text{WPM} \times \text{Comprehension \%}$.

ΔERR (Growth Delta): True cognitive growth measured between Form A (Baseline) and Form B (Re-Test).

2. Pedagogical Rationale & Research Base

Middle school reading interventions often fall into two extremes: physical speed reading without understanding, or passive reading without focus pacing. The Cognitive Reading Pacer bridges these modalities by targeting saccadic eye movement efficiency and working memory retention.

  • Focus Shutter (Module 1): Eliminates line re-reading (subvocal regressions) by highlighting one line at a time in a soft yellow lens while dimming peripheral distractors.
  • Morpho-Visual Flasher (Module 2): Uses Optimal Anchor Point (OAP) targeting underneath a red crosshair, expanding visual span from single letters to 3-word phrase chunks.
  • PQRST Active Reading (Module 3): Structures metacognitive recall through Preview, Question, Read, Summary Recite, and Test verification.

3. Weekly 3-Session Implementation Protocol

Session Target Module Duration Focus Goal
Day 1 Standardized Sprint + M1 15 mins Establish baseline ERR on Form A, set Focus Shutter to +10 WPM above baseline.
Day 2 Module 2 (Visual Flasher) 12 mins Complete 25 flashes at 350ms setting. Lock eye gaze on red target crosshair.
Day 3 Module 3 + Form B Re-Test 15 mins Complete PQRST active read and execute Form B Re-Test sprint to calculate ΔERR growth.

4. Lexile Band Calibration Reference

Foundational Band (400L – 550L)

Short sentences, high frequency words. Ideal for 5th grade fluency building or struggling readers.

Intermediate Band (600L – 750L)

Standard 6th/7th grade benchmark. Balanced compound sentences with domain vocabulary.

Advanced Band (800L – 950L)

7th/8th grade honors target. Multi-clause sentences, high-level vocabulary, non-fiction domain topics.

Mastery Band (1000L+)

High school readiness & IXL top band. Complex technical syntax and abstract concepts.
