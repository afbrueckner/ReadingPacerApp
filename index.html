1. Key Metrics & Definitions

WPM: Words Per Minute (Physical tracking speed).

Comprehension %: Quiz accuracy score.

ERR: Effective Reading Rate ($\text{WPM} \times \text{Comprehension \%}$).

ΔERR: Growth delta measured between Form A and Form B.
