📖 Educator & Parent Field Guide

Cognitive Reading Pacer — Field Manual

Catholic Middle School Edition • Research-Backed Fluency & Comprehension Protocol

1. Key Metrics & Definitions

WPM (Words Per Minute): Pure physical tracking speed across lines of text ($W / T_{sec} \times 60$).

Comprehension %: Accuracy score on post-reading verification quiz ($100\%$ or $50\%$).

ERR (Effective Reading Rate): Calculated as $\text{WPM} \times \text{Comprehension \%}$. This core metric prevents skimming by penalizing fast reading without retention.

ΔERR (Growth Delta): True cognitive growth measured between standardized Form A (Baseline) and Form B (Re-Test) benchmarks.

The Golden Rule of ERR: A student reading at 300 WPM with 50% comprehension has an ERR of 150. A student reading at 180 WPM with 100% comprehension has an ERR of 180. Higher comprehension always wins.

2. Lexile Bands vs. RTI Support Tiers

To avoid confusion with school Multi-Tiered System of Support (MTSS/RTI) frameworks, the app separates instructional support tiers from text readability bands:

RTI Support Tiers (School Level)

• Tier 1: Universal core classroom instruction ($100\%$ of students).

• Tier 2: Targeted small-group intervention ($10–15\%$ needing pacer support).

• Tier 3: Intensive individualized intervention ($5\%$ specialized support).

App Lexile Reading Bands

• Foundational Band (400L–550L): Grade 4–5 readability.

• Intermediate Band (600L–750L): On-grade Grade 6–7 readability.

• Advanced Band (800L–950L): Grade 8 / Honors readability.

• Mastery Band (1000L+): High School / College Ready readability.

3. The 4 Pacing Modules

1. Standardized Diagnostic (Tab 1)

Establishes true baseline ERR using pre-calibrated static benchmark texts (Form A/B) matching IXL Lexile bands.

2. Accelerator Focus Shutter (Module 1)

Eliminates line regression and sub-vocalization by pacing eyes forward with a moving highlight shutter window.

3. Morpho-Visual Flasher (Module 2)

Expands peripheral visual attention span and foveal fixation width using phrase flashes centered on anchor target letters.

4. PQRST Active Reader (Module 3)

Guides Preview, Question, Read, State/Recite, and Test steps for high comprehension retention on faith-aligned AI topics.

4. Recommended 15-Minute Classroom Protocol

  1. Diagnostic / Re-Test (3 Mins - Mondays/Fridays): Complete Form A (Day 1) or Form B (Progress Check) on Tab 1 to log ERR.
  2. Ocular Span Warmup (3 Mins): Run 20–30 flashes on Module 2 (Visual Flasher) to expand peripheral fixation span.
  3. Speed Pacing (5 Mins): Set Module 1 Focus Shutter to +20 WPM above baseline on a custom topic (e.g., St. Patrick, Solar System).
  4. Active Recall & Quiz (4 Mins): Complete Module 3 (PQRST Reader) preview, read, and verification quiz.

5. Teacher Troubleshooting & Intervention Matrix

Observed Behavior Root Cognitive Cause Recommended Action
High Speed / Low Quiz Score The Skimmer: Eye movement outpaces cognitive comprehension. Lower Module 1 WPM by 30 points. Require 90%+ quiz score before increasing speed.
Slow Speed / 100% Quiz Score The Perfectionist: Sub-vocalizes every word; frequent line regression. Increase Module 1 Focus Shutter speed by +20 WPM to force comfortable forward movement.
Consistent +30 ERR Growth Mastery Achieved: Expanded optical span and pacing capacity. Promote Student: Switch Lexile selector to the next higher reading band!
